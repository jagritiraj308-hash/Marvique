Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Marvique Tiles & Marble respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you use this website, contact us or place an order, and how that information is used.

Information we collect

  • Your name, phone number, city and message when you send an enquiry.
  • Delivery details you share when placing an order online or on WhatsApp.
  • Order and payment status when you pay through our online checkout.
  • Products you save to your wishlist, stored in your own browser.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and help you choose the right product.
  • To confirm, prepare and deliver your order.
  • To keep a record of payments and order status.
  • To improve the products and information shown on this website.

Payments

Online payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank credentials. When online payment is not available, orders can be completed on WhatsApp and paid at the showroom or by an agreed method.

Services we use

  • Firebase (Google) for website hosting, sign-in and product data.
  • Cloudinary for storing and delivering product images.
  • Razorpay for online payments, when enabled.
  • WhatsApp for enquiries and order conversations you start.
  • Google Maps to show our showroom location.

Data stored in your browser

Your wishlist and basic contact details are saved in your browser’s local storage so the site is easier to use next time. You can remove this at any time by clearing your browser data or using the “Clear Wishlist” button.

Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. Details are shared only with the services needed to run the website and complete your order, or where required by law.
